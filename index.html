Tonight's paper

You're holding a stranger's tab

One Tip from the local paper. One receipt with the currency mark ripped off. Somewhere in the world, that was lunch.

The numbers never get converted. Whatever they paid locally is what you see. Yen stays yen-sized. Places that use cents keep decimals. We only hide the symbol.

Two wrong guesses and the tab becomes lunch. Two more and it becomes dinner with a drink, something that actually belongs to that city.

Guess the city. Six tries. Hotter or colder. No map.
